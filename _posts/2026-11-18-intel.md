---

### 1. Ring-Fence Holiday & Living Cash Flow First

Black Friday and December holiday spending create a predictable, short-term cash drain. The cardinal rule of rational retail investing during a drawdown is **never risking money you need in the next 6 to 12 months**.

* **Protect your emergency fund and holiday budget:** If buying Bitcoin requires putting Black Friday gifts on a high-interest credit card or dipping into rent/bill reserves, stop buying. Carrying a 20%+ APR credit card balance wipes out any mathematically realistic short-term gain.
* **Define a fixed "dry powder" cap:** Decide on a strict, disposable dollar limit for the remainder of Q4 that assumes your holiday expenses are already 100% funded in cash.

### 2. Why Over-Indexing on Cost Basis Can Be a Trap

Yesterday, I myself added $500 of BTC to my account and it dropped my average cost basis by $1,000, which feels productive, and mathematically it lowered my break-even threshold. However, small investors often fall into **cost-basis chasing**—throwing disproportionate capital at a falling asset simply to watch the average price number tick down. Discipline is the key, so going forward my approach will be as follows:

* **Focus on position sizing, not just average price:** As your total stack grows, each additional $500 moves your average cost basis less and less (diminishing marginal impact).
* **Respect overhead supply:** Because 73% of recent buyers are underwater and U.S. spot ETFs have seen heavy net outflows amid Fed rate-hike jitters, bounces toward the **$68,000–$75,600** zone are likely to meet resistance from trapped buyers selling to break even. Expect a choppy, multi-week or multi-month consolidation rather than an instant V-shaped recovery.

### 3. Practical Execution Rules for Today

* **Use Automated DCA or Staggered Limit Orders:** Instead of guessing whether $62,000 or July's ~$57,750 low is the exact bottom, deploy your pre-set budget in small, fixed tranches (e.g., weekly buys) or place resting limit bids at key structural support levels below spot.
* **Zero Leverage:** Over $2 billion in leveraged positions were wiped out during the mid-November drop alone. Spot-only ownership eliminates liquidation risk and lets you wait out macro headwinds on your own timeline.
* **Self-Custody & Security Hygiene:** Drawdowns historically coincide with exchange stress and phishing spikes. If you are accumulating for a multi-year horizon, move meaningful balances off exchanges into cold/hardware storage where you control the keys.
* **Tax-Loss Harvesting (If Applicable):** If you hold underwater lots in a taxable brokerage or ETF wrapper—and have realized capital gains elsewhere in 2026—selling high-cost-basis lots to harvest the tax loss (while observing applicable wash-sale rules for securities/ETFs) can turn paper losses into real tax savings without abandoning your long-term thesis.

Good luck! The market always springs back. The downturn we have seen has been in every other market as well.
