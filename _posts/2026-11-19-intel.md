Key Highlights

- Latest Reading (Nov 2026): 6.3 (vs. 20.9 prior and ~20.1–20.6 expected), pointing to a marked slowdown in regional factory expansion while staying slightly above zero.
- Prior Month (Oct 2026): 20.9 (down from 37.8 in September), with new orders at 4.3, shipments at 15.3, and employment at 9.1.
- What It Measures: Compiled monthly since May 1968 by the Federal Reserve Bank of Philadelphia's Manufacturing Business Outlook Survey from roughly 250 manufacturers in the Third Federal Reserve District (Pennsylvania, New Jersey, and Delaware).
- How to Read It: Diffusion index calculated as the percentage of firms reporting increases minus those reporting decreases. Readings above 0 signal expanding/improving conditions; readings below 0 signal contraction.
- Market Context: A steeper-than-expected drop can weigh on U.S. dollar sentiment and prompt closer scrutiny of broader industrial and Fed policy outlooks.
