# Bitcoin Weekday Intel

## Strategic News

Macroeconomic liquidity conditions enter the Thanksgiving holiday week under heightened monetary policy tension following Friday's cross-asset risk-off flush and safe-haven Treasury rally. On Friday, benchmark 2-year U.S. Treasury yields fell more than 11 basis points to close below 4.65% while 10-year yields dropped over 10 basis points to 4.94% as investors rotated into sovereign paper amidst heavy U.S. tech equity selling and widening high-yield credit spreads (Bloomberg U.S. High Yield OAS blew out 23 bps to 367 bps, its widest since early 2025). Despite overnight remarks from Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent cautioning against tightening into an energy supply shock, markets still assign a **98% probability** to a 25 bps Federal Reserve rate hike at the December 8–9 FOMC meeting, even as anticipated 2027 tightening expectations have moderated. Ahead of Saturday's pre-FOMC blackout window, desks are closely monitoring afternoon remarks from New York Fed President John Williams (21:00 UTC / 4:00 PM ET) alongside today's $69.0 Billion 2-year Treasury note auction (18:00 UTC).

Direct sovereign balance-sheet liquidity support enters a scheduled Thanksgiving holiday pause this week. Following the formal settlement on Friday, November 20, of the Treasury's $4.0 Billion nominal 7-year to 10-year debt buyback, no secondary liquidity support buybacks are scheduled during the current holiday week. The Treasury’s next scheduled operation will be announced on Tuesday, December 1, ahead of a major **$12.5 Billion** nominal coupon buyback on December 2.

## Institutional ETF Flows & Liquidity Absorption

U.S. Spot Bitcoin ETFs suffered their third-largest weekly outflow since launch during the week ending Friday, November 20, shedding **-$1.80 Billion** across the five trading sessions and marking four consecutive weeks of institutional net redemptions. Friday alone saw **-$669.00 Million** in net withdrawals—extending the daily outflow streak to four straight sessions—with redemptions broadening beyond BlackRock’s **IBIT** into Fidelity’s **FBTC** and Grayscale’s **GBTC**, while Morgan Stanley’s **MSBT** stood out as a rare holdout logging modest inflows.

* **Weekly & Monthly Flow Context:** Cumulative November net outflows have now reached approximately **-$3.50 Billion** through November 20 (with IBIT accounting for ~85% of the monthly drawdown), placing November within striking distance of June 2026's record monthly outflow (-$4.51B).
* **Structural Holdings vs. Secondary Tape:** Despite near-term macro redemptions, newly compiled Q3 Form 13F filings (2,166 institutional filers) confirm that institutional ETP ownership reached an all-time high of **27.6%** of total ETP bitcoin holdings at quarter-end—led by net quarterly additions from Wells Fargo (+14,636 BTC equivalent), Brevan Howard (+8,606 BTC), Bank of America (+7,201 BTC), and Millennium Management (+7,114 BTC).
* **Secondary Depth Absorption:** Passive spot limit bids stepped in aggressively over the weekend between **$55,100 and $58,800** to absorb forced liquidations, allowing spot price to reclaim the **$61,000** handle without secondary ETF desk pressure over the weekend.

On-chain liquidity maps define **$53,000–$58,500** as the primary structural accumulation shelf, guarding against a stiff overhead recovery hurdle between **$62,500 and $65,000**.
