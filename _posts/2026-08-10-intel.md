## General Market Summary

Over the last 24 hours, Bitcoin completed a decisive weekend consolidation, trading between an intraday low of **$64,650.00** and an intraday high of **$65,220.00** before settling near **$65,044.88**. Following Friday's weak labor market data, spot buyers systematically absorbed Short-Term Holder break-even supply, driving price across the $65,000 psychological barrier with minimal slippage.

To the upside, a sustained daily close above **$65,250** will officially exhaust the remaining Short-Term Holder cost-basis overhang, opening a thinned-out liquidity corridor toward the 100-day EMA near **$66,900**. With macro rate-cut tailwinds locked in and institutional ETF flows accelerating, market structure heading into the second week of August remains bullish and spot-anchored.
