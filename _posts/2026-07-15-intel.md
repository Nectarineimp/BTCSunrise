Most commentators on X focus entirely on "spot" buying and selling—retail users clicking buttons on an app. But the big game is played in the derivatives market (futures and options) and through institutional OTC (Over-the-Counter) desks.

* **The Bid Walls:** When giant corporate entities or systemic market-makers want to defend a bottom, they don't buy directly on public exchanges where you can see it on a retail chart. They set up massive **iceberg orders** and hidden limit bids through prime brokers like Coinbase Prime or Fidelity.

This systematic short-hedging creates highly resilient, synthetic floors under the market. It acts like a massive shock absorber; even if a panic-seller dumps, these massive institutional passive buy blocks just soak up the sell pressure without the price moving an inch.

Overall, this looks like a true structural change in the landscape for the week.
