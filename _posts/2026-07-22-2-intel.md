## Strategic News

The global macroeconomic environment continues to adapt to a structural easing narrative as fixed-income desks recalibrate following July's disinflationary Consumer Price Index (CPI) print. Committee discussions ahead of the August congressional recess are focused on refining statutory jurisdiction between the CFTC and SEC while finalizing ethics and disclosure frameworks. Prediction markets continue to reflect elevated probabilities of a full Senate floor vote. Establishing a clear federal statutory framework that classifies established digital assets as commodities is viewed by major financial institutions as a primary catalyst required to unlock broader sovereign and pension fund allocations.
