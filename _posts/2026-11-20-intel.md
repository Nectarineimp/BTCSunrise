## Institutional ETF Flows & Liquidity Absorption

Combined net redemptions from November 17 through November 19 have now reached **-$1.13 Billion** (-$239.9M, -$306.5M, and -$582.19M), removing a critical marginal demand source while spot prices breached key technical support.

* **Net Daily Flow (Nov 19):** -$582.19 Million (~9,440 BTC net redeemed).

* **AUM & Float Impact:** Across the trailing month, aggregate spot Bitcoin ETF assets under management have contracted from ~$107.8 Billion to roughly **$87.5 Billion** (reflecting both price repricing and ~$3.5B in cumulative net outflows since late October), leaving secondary spot order books reliant on passive limit bid depth between **$60,000 and $61,500** to absorb selling flow.

## General Market Summary

Over the past 24 hours, Bitcoin broke below its multi-day **$62,000** defense band, sliding from **$63,702.42** to touch an overnight four-month low of **$61,353.68** before stabilizing near **$61,665.19** during European morning trade on Friday.

Microstructure telemetry confirms that the breakdown was driven by converging institutional and cross-asset pressures: U.S. Spot Bitcoin ETFs suffered **-$582.19 Million** in net outflows on Thursday—led by a **-$541.10 Million** redemption from BlackRock’s IBIT—while U.S. equities logged broad losses (S&P 500 down six straight sessions; Nasdaq futures breaking below their 200-day moving average) and the U.S. Dollar Index climbed to a 19-month high ahead of December FOMC rate-hike expectations. The breach of $62,000 triggered **$194.50 Million** in Bitcoin futures liquidations (83% longs), yet derivatives indicators show an orderly deleveraging rather than panic short-stacking: open interest contracted to **$41.49 Billion**, the **Predicted Funding Rate (+0.0021%)** remains flat at an unlevered baseline, and the **Long/Short Ratio (1.02)** sits nearly balanced. With the Crypto Fear & Greed Index pinned at **11 (Extreme Fear)** and roughly half of circulating supply sitting in unrealized loss, spot price is directly engaging the upper edge of the **$59,500–$61,000** liquidation and bid cluster. Holding above **$61,350** (and defending the **$60,000** psychological floor) into the weekend is critical to prevent a deeper structural sweep, while bulls must reclaim **$62,370–$63,750** to neutralize immediate downside momentum.
